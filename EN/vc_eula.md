# Voice Channel (VC) Rules

Last revised: September 10, 2026

~ For an environment where everyone can interact with peace of mind ~

## Introduction

These are supplemental rules that support the server's Terms of Service with regard to VC use. To realize the server's philosophy of "a community where we can all reach a 'Medetashi (happily ever after)' with love and compassion," we have compiled the manners and morals that help make VC a better place for interaction.

At its core, VC is "**a place for everyone to communicate by voice and deepen their connections**."

So that new members and those who join less often can also feel free to take part, we expect all members to help run VC with "love and compassion."

## Basic principles of VC use

1. **VC is for communication.** The purpose of VC is to "talk" and "interact" with other members through voice chat. Try to make sure everyone taking part can enjoy talking together by voice.
2. **Do not occupy or monopolize VC.** Place more importance on "what you do there" and "how you interact" than on staying in VC for a long time. What matters is not extending your time but raising the quality of interaction. (**The dedicated AFK channel is excluded.**)
3. **Be welcoming to new members, too.** All participants, please work together to create an atmosphere in which new members and those who join less often can feel free to take part.

## Things to keep in mind when using VC

* **Join VC only when you need to.** Join with a purpose, such as "I'm about to do X" or "I want to talk with someone or hear what others are saying." If you run out of things to do or are switching to something else, **make sure to leave VC**.
* **Leaving your account idle is strictly prohibited.** Staying in VC with only your account left idle in order to extend your usage time is prohibited (except in the dedicated AFK channel). Keeping a VC going with just your account while both your microphone and speakers are muted is, in itself, occupying the VC.
* **Hand over hosting properly.** When the host (the person who started a personal VC; referred to as the "owner" by the bot) leaves that VC, the host should close the channel, or someone who stays on to continue the call should take over the host permissions. You can have owner permissions transferred to you with `/voice shutoku` (only when the original owner is not present).

## Prohibited conduct (examples)

* Staying put in the channel instead of leaving while both your microphone and speakers are muted.
* Staying put in the channel instead of leaving while you are away from your device for a long time, such as when commuting to school or work.

(In both cases, the dedicated AFK channel is excluded.)

## Conduct to avoid (examples)

* Staying in VC for a long time without communicating with anyone, regardless of whether your microphone is on or off.
* Intentionally continuing to stay for a long time with the aim of "extending your time" rather than communicating.

Note: If you have muted only your microphone and are listening to the conversation (listening only), this does not count as idling.

## Relationship with existing rules

Violating these rules may make you subject to progressive warnings under Article 6 (Penalties) of the Terms of Service.

**Note: Where malicious occupation of VC is found, or where there is no improvement even after multiple warnings from moderators, a severe penalty may be applied regardless of the number of warnings (strikes).**

In addition, under Article 3 of the Terms of Service, recording calls, recording screen shares, and externally reposting or publishing such recordings are prohibited, regardless of consent or management approval.

## Closing

The purpose of these rules is not "to punish anyone" but **"to create a VC environment where everyone can feel safe and enjoy interacting with one another."** Let's all build a good community together while holding on to "love and compassion," the server's philosophy.

If anything is unclear or you have any questions, feel free to consult a moderator or administrator through Ticket Tool or other means.

Note: These rules may be changed without prior notice depending on how the server is used and other circumstances.
