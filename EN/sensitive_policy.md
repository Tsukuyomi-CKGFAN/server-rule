# Sensitive Content Policy

Last revised: September 10, 2026

## Overview

This document sets out the basic policy on the handling of sensitive content on ツクヨミ-超かぐや姫！ファン鯖-.

This server's rules on sensitive content consist of the following three documents:

- **Sensitive Content Policy** (this document): the basic policy on the handling of sensitive content
- **Sensitive Content Regulation**: the rules that set out the definition of sensitive content, the scope of application, management's judgment, and related matters
- **Sensitive Content Guidelines**: concrete examples that serve as a guide when judging whether something can be posted

## Background

Management has already been treating the intentional use of terms from the work in a sexual context (as euphemisms) as a violation of the prohibition on sensitive content, and has been speaking to the users concerned individually or issuing strikes.
However, because concrete criteria like these had not been written down, there was a concern that some people might be left uneasy, wondering, "Is this okay to post?"
We have therefore now put management's criteria for judgment into writing as the Sensitive Content Regulation and the Sensitive Content Guidelines, so that users can continue to enjoy interacting with fellow fans without worry.

## Basic policy

- On this server, content that falls under what is commonly called R-18 or NSFW is collectively referred to as "sensitive content," and both are equally prohibited, whether in posts or in conduct.
- Spoiler tags are not a means of permitting sensitive content. Prohibited content may not be posted whether or not it is hidden by a spoiler tag.
- Management determines whether something is sensitive content through an overall assessment based on the Sensitive Content Regulation and the Sensitive Content Guidelines.
