# Sensitive Content Guidelines

Last revised: September 10, 2026

## Overview

Based on the Sensitive Content Regulation, this document uses concrete examples to set out, as clearly as possible, the criteria for judging what may be posted with regard to the handling of sexual expression, grotesque expression, anthropomorphic animal characters, robot depictions, euphemisms, and the like.
For the definition of sensitive content and management's judgment, see the Sensitive Content Regulation; for the basic policy, see the Sensitive Content Policy.

## Examples of R-18 content

- Full nudity, or a state close to it
- Exposed genitals, whether or not censored with a mosaic or by other means
- Sex acts, or depictions that clearly evoke them
- Depictions of sexual devices being used
- Penetration, or depictions that clearly suggest it, regardless of what is inserted
- Depictions of urination or excrement
- Depictions of devices intended primarily for sexual use, such as vibrators, dildos, and masturbation sleeves, as well as adult-product packaging or adult toys on their own, regardless of whether there is any sexual context
- Depictions of objects whose shape, composition, or staging clearly evokes sexual use
- Expression that intentionally presents items not meant for sexual use as substitutes for sexual devices, including food such as cucumbers or bananas and everyday items such as pens, brushes, or containers with bumpy or ridged surfaces
- Staging with a clear sexual context, such as pressing something against the mouth, chest, crotch, buttocks, or similar areas, suggesting insertion, or excessively emphasizing those areas

## Examples of NSFW content

In addition to all content falling under R-18 above, NSFW includes the following.

### Sexual or provocative expression

- Underwear, swimwear, or bunny suits that expose an extreme amount of skin, and outfits that expose the chest or buttocks to a marked degree
- Outfits that cling to the skin and strongly show the body's contours or parts, such as school swimsuits or rubber suits
- See-through clothing, or depictions in which the skin or body lines show through because of wetness
- Clothing, poses, or staging that is sexual or looks sexual
- Excessive sweat or skin shine
- Strong expression of extreme sexual preferences
- Strong emphasis on the chest, crotch, or buttocks using hands, clothing, objects, or the like, even if the private parts themselves are not visible
- Bodily fluids, drool, or the like emphasized in a sexual context
- Depictions of liquid that clearly evokes sexual use, shown clinging to a person or object
- Fetish expression using restraints, collars, gags, or the like
- Anything else judged to be clearly sexual or provocative expression

### Grotesque or extreme expression

- Serious depictions of torture (for example, hanging, water torture, or extreme spanking)
- Depictions of bloodshed or pieces of flesh
- Realistic zombies or depictions of dissection
- Severed limbs or depictions of severe loss of body parts
- Direct depictions of accidents or similar incidents involving human bodies
- Extreme depictions of filth, such as vomiting, incontinence, or defecation

## Non-human expression (animals, robots, etc.)

### Anthropomorphic animal characters and animals

- If the entire body is covered in sufficient fur and the depiction does not give a strong sexual impression, it is, in principle, not classified as NSFW.
- If the fur is thin, the body shape is close to human, the chest, buttocks, crotch, or similar areas are emphasized, or the depiction looks sexual, it is classified as NSFW.
- Even if the character does not appear fully nude, the depiction is treated as NSFW if thin fur or emphasis on the body leads it to be perceived as sexual expression.

### Robots, cyborgs, and inanimate objects

The following are treated as sensitive content even if the subject is in fact artificial or inanimate, including cyborg (android) depictions like the future Kaguya in the work:

- Depictions that give the subject human bodily characteristics and come across as sexual (for example, excessive emphasis on specific parts such as the chest or buttocks, or depiction of private parts)
- Depictions of sex acts, or of anything that clearly evokes them, even if the depiction itself is not sexual

## Euphemisms

Internet-meme-style uses have been seen in which lines or proper nouns from the work are turned into euphemisms with sexual connotations. However, under this server's management policy, malicious use that seriously undermines respect for the characters and the world of the original work is, in principle, prohibited.

### Examples of prohibited use

- Posting terms or song titles from the work that have been intentionally altered or punned into sexual words (for example, changing an original song title into a sexual word).
- Making heavy use of terms from the work as euphemisms where the context clearly shows the aim is to enjoy sexual topics or dirty jokes.

### Examples of permitted use

Using greetings from the work such as "かぐやっほー！" or names such as "なかよしのやつ" purely to talk about the work or in everyday communication. (Note: Even if such words have a euphemistic side, they are considered fine in the ordinary flow of conversation.)

Note: Even mild expressions where it is hard to tell whether they count as euphemisms may be subject to penalties if they are persistently repeated, or if management determines that the context is one that other participants would find uncomfortable.

## Closing

These are only examples. Note that prohibited content may not be posted even if it is hidden with a spoiler tag (see the Sensitive Content Regulation).
If you are unsure, or if you come across content that makes you uncomfortable, please do not hesitate to consult a moderator or lead moderator.
