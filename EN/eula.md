# Server Philosophy

Last revised: October 1, 2026

This server was created for fans of "超かぐや姫！ (Cosmic Princess Kaguya!)" and related content to interact and enjoy themselves safely. Our goal is to build, all together, a community where everyone follows common sense, acts with love and compassion, and can reach a "Medetashi (happily ever after)."

---

# Terms of Service
## Article 1 (Agreement to Terms)
All users of this server (hereinafter "users") are deemed to have agreed to these terms.
**If a minor uses this server, they are deemed to have obtained prior consent from a parent or guardian.**
These terms may be changed without prior notice due to factors including server usage, changing circumstances, or changes to official rules.
In principle, penalties will not be applied retroactively for conduct that occurred before a change to these terms. This does not apply, however, to malicious cases that management determines to be seriously harmful to the community.

## Article 2 (Compliance with Platform Rules)
Users must comply with Discord's official [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), as well as the [Derivative Works Guidelines](https://www.cho-kaguyahime.com/special/detail.html?id=1024) issued by the official "超かぐや姫！" team (X: [@Cho_KaguyaHime](https://x.com/Cho_KaguyaHime)). Violations of these rules will also be handled strictly on this server.

## Article 3 (Prohibited Acts)
Users must not engage in the following prohibited conduct (including equivalent expressions or settings), not only in text or image posts and in what they say, but also in **all settings that other users may see on this server, such as usernames, avatars (icons), custom statuses, and About Me**.

* **Damaging the integrity of the work**
  * Posting content that, in management's judgment, objectively disrupts the harmony of the community, such as posts that significantly defile the characters or the world-building, malicious alterations, or crossovers with other works that significantly damage the work's image.
* **Posting inappropriate content**
  * Posting sensitive content such as R-18 (adult content, in any form) or grotesque material. Also, posting discriminatory content, political or religious solicitation, or content that violates laws (including making others view such content through links or URLs to external sites). See the Sensitive Content Policy for the basic policy on sensitive content, the Sensitive Content Regulation for its definition, and the Sensitive Content Guidelines for concrete examples.
* **Inappropriate use of AI-generated content**
  * Please post generative-AI content in the `AI作品` channel.
  * Within that channel, such content is in principle unrestricted so long as it does not violate Discord's Terms of Service or the Derivative Works Guidelines. Indiscriminate posting outside the dedicated channel is, in principle, prohibited.
* **Concealment and nuisance conduct**
  * For the purpose of destroying evidence or obstructing management: deleting or altering (editing) an inappropriate post immediately after posting it (so-called "instant deletion" or "instant editing"); deleting past inappropriate posts in bulk; or, after posting an external link containing inappropriate content, immediately deleting or altering the linked content to destroy evidence. Ordinary editing or deletion, such as correcting typos or withdrawing an accidentally sent image, does not fall under this rule. Note: Edit and deletion logs are recorded by the system.
* **Misuse or overuse of spoiler functions**
  * Indiscriminate use of Discord's spoiler function to hide statements.
  * Even when statements are hidden with the spoiler function, depending on their content (such as concealing abusive language or rule-violating content, or meaningless overuse), they may be deemed disruptive conduct (trolling) and subject to penalties. Please use the function responsibly.
* **Privacy violations**
  * Posting personal information (such as names, addresses, telephone numbers, or face photographs), whether your own or a third party's, or attempting to identify a third party's personal information.
  * Administrators accept no responsibility whatsoever for disadvantages or damages arising from the handling of personal information.
* **Dissemination of unofficial or improperly obtained information**
  * Bringing into this server leaks of important information that has not yet been officially announced, data obtained through analysis, or other unofficial information obtained by improper means, as well as spreading such information.
* **Disruption of communication**
  * Spam (repeated posting), unauthorized mass mentions (`@everyone` / `@here`), defamation or abuse of others, excessive fighting, persistently picking arguments in disregard of management's instructions, harassment through emoji reactions, or repeated individual mentions for harassment.
* **Unauthorized advertising and solicitation**
  * Unauthorized advertising of other servers, or of activities run by individuals or other organizations (such as social media, streaming platforms, or paid services of any kind), except in permitted channels. This includes not only directly posting URLs but also indirect advertising, such as directing users to search for them. Also, creating another server bearing this server's name without permission (prior permission required).
* **Bringing in external disputes or other problems**
  * Bringing private disputes, conflicts, or other problems that occurred on external social media, in DMs, on other servers, or otherwise outside this server into channels, threads, voice channels, profiles, tickets, or any other function on this server, and raising them as a topic of conversation.
  * Requesting that management determine another party's responsibility, demand an apology or compensation from them, penalize them, mediate, find facts, or otherwise resolve such matters. This does not apply to reports of serious safety violations as defined in Article 4.
* **Poaching users to external servers**
  * Encouraging participation in an external server in text channels or threads, or soliciting or directing users to an external server in voice conversations.
  * Posting another server's invitation URL or a means of joining it in this server's channels, or calling on others to join it.
  * Any other conduct, regardless of method, such as misuse of profile features (status, etc.) or individual DMs, that moderators or administrators determine to be poaching or solicitation.
* **Damaging the reputation of this server or external servers**
  * Comparing other servers with this server and making statements or taking actions that disparage this server's atmosphere, management, members, or the like. Also, making statements or taking actions that damage the reputation of another community. This does not include a private report of a serious safety violation under Article 4.
* **Inappropriate use of voice channels or music bots**
  * Playing noisy MADs or inappropriate content, continuously transmitting unpleasant noise from an intentionally misconfigured microphone, or playing any other sound or audio source that may make others uncomfortable. Also, recording calls or screen shares, regardless of consent or management approval, and externally reposting or publishing such recordings.
* **Other conduct management determines inappropriate**
  * Even where a matter is not expressly addressed in these terms, or where the interpretation of their wording is in doubt, the management team may, at its discretion, impose penalties or other measures for conduct it determines to be inappropriate from the standpoint of preserving server order, member safety, and the integrity of the work. Except for the limited reconsideration under Article 6, management's determination is not open to objection.

### Conduct concerning external servers
**Note: clarification regarding partner servers**
> Servers other than those officially recognized and announced as partner servers by this server, including servers that this server's administrators or moderators belong to or are involved in, are treated as independent and entirely unrelated to this server. An administrator's or moderator's personal membership does not mean this server recommends or guarantees that server.

## Article 4 (Handling of User Disputes and External Conduct)
Administrators accept no responsibility for personal conflicts between users or other disputes that occur outside this server, and will not be involved in determining another party's responsibility, mediating, demanding apologies or compensation, or otherwise resolving problems occurring externally. For ordinary external disputes, please use the reporting or consultation services of the relevant platform or another appropriate point of contact.

Even if a problem occurred externally, exposing others, attacking, soliciting, or fueling disruption within this server will be handled under Articles 3 and 6 as a violation of these terms on this server.

**However, this does not apply to threats, disclosure or identification of personal information, sexual exploitation, illegal conduct, or other conduct that may cause concrete and serious harm to a user's safety ("serious safety violations").** A user who has been harmed by or has witnessed such conduct may, after protecting themselves as far as possible by blocking or other means, promptly report it to management through a private ticket.

Screenshots, URLs, reposted material, personal information, sensitive information, and any other evidence concerning conduct outside this server must not be submitted, reposted, or shared on this server, including in tickets; management will not receive, store, or share them. Even in urgent cases, please use the reporting services of Discord or another relevant platform, or an appropriate point of contact such as a public authority. To ensure safety within this server, management may impose provisional contact restrictions, but will not make findings of fact, issue warnings or penalties, mediate, or negotiate concerning the external conduct itself. Management will give due consideration to the reporter's safety and confidentiality where possible.

## Article 5 (Handling of Accounts and Other Matters)
* In principle, each user may use only one account on this server. However, **the use of multiple accounts (sub-accounts) due to circumstances such as a user's environment is left to everyone's good judgment. No prior application is required, but if abuse, such as trolling or evading penalties, is confirmed, or if a rule violation is confirmed on any account the user holds, strict penalties will be imposed, including on related accounts.**
* A person sharing their screen assumes all responsibility for its content.
* Images used as profile icons are assumed to be images that each user has the rights to, uses in accordance with applicable guidelines, or has permission to use. Administrators accept no responsibility whatsoever for conflicts, disadvantages, or problems arising from their use.
* In principle, administrators do not intervene in DMs between users. However, if exchanges that may cause harm to users of other servers are confirmed, they may take appropriate measures.

**About spoilers:** Because a certain period has passed since the work's release, this server does not restrict conversations containing spoilers for the main story. Users who have not watched it or wish to avoid spoilers should exercise care at their own discretion.

## Article 6 (Penalties)
Violations of these terms will result in the following progressive measures according to the cumulative number of strikes. For system-management reasons, a strike, once issued, will in principle not be reset. The number of strikes issued at once may be increased or decreased after consultation among moderators and administrators.

* Strike 1: Strict caution or warning from the management team
* Strike 2: 10-minute timeout (temporary restriction on posting and speaking)
* Strike 3: 50-minute timeout
* Strike 4: 1-day timeout
* Strike 5: Softban
* Strike 6: Permanent ban

Violations of Article 3, "Bringing in external disputes or other problems," are, in principle, subject to the strikes above. Management will not issue a warning or penalty for external conduct on the sole basis of a report of a serious safety violation concerning that conduct.

### Limited reconsideration of penalties

A user who receives a penalty may, only within 7 days from the date of the notice of the penalty, request reconsideration through a private ticket on the grounds of a mistaken finding, impersonation, procedural defect, or a conflict of interest involving the staff member responsible. In a reconsideration, a staff member who was not involved in the penalty will review only the facts and procedure; mediation of external disputes, re-argument of the penalty itself, and the submission or sharing of additional external evidence will not be accepted.

*Notwithstanding the above, where any of the following is confirmed, the management team may, at its discretion, impose an immediate permanent ban regardless of the number of warnings (strikes): violations of Discord's rules; serious desecration of the work; malicious harassment (such as instant deletion or instant editing); **using or possessing multiple accounts (sub-accounts) to avoid the accumulation of penalties or to evade bans, or a group of several people colluding to systematically spread out or repeat prohibited conduct to avoid accumulating penalties**; or serious problems falling under the catch-all provision of Article 3.*
