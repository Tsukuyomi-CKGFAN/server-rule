# Sensitive Content Regulation

Last revised: September 10, 2026

## Overview

This document sets out in writing the regulation (rules) on sensitive content on ツクヨミ-超かぐや姫！ファン鯖-.
It specifies the definition of sensitive content, the difference between NSFW and R-18 and the criteria for judging them, the scope of application, and management's judgment.
The basic policy is set out in the Sensitive Content Policy, and concrete examples of how sexual expression, grotesque expression, anthropomorphic animal characters, robot depictions, euphemisms, and the like are handled are compiled in the Sensitive Content Guidelines.

## Scope of application

The Sensitive Content Policy, this regulation, and the Sensitive Content Guidelines apply to all activity and expression on this server, including text, images, video, audio, external links, emoji, reactions, and user profiles (such as icons and statuses).

## What is sensitive content?

On this server, content that falls under what is commonly called R-18 or NSFW is collectively defined as "sensitive content," and both are equally prohibited, whether in posts or in conduct.

Within sensitive content, NSFW is short for "Not safe for work" and means "better not to view at work."
Although the term refers specifically to work, it generally means content that is best not viewed in places such as schools, trains, and public facilities, not just at work.

## Why R-18 and NSFW are defined separately

Both are prohibited on this server, but they are clearly defined separately because they differ in how objectively they can be judged.

- **R-18**: Sexual content inappropriate for minors, such as nudity or explicit sex acts, that can easily be judged objectively as unacceptable.
- **NSFW**: All content falling under R-18 above, plus content that does not fall under R-18 but where viewers can easily disagree about whether it is acceptable, such as excessive exposure or fetish expression.

So that users are not left wondering where the line is, the borderline of what is prohibited as NSFW on this server is set out in writing in the Sensitive Content Guidelines.

## Management's final judgment (disclaimer)

The contents of the Sensitive Content Guidelines are representative examples of the criteria used for judgment.
Even expressions that slip past the letter of the rules, or mild expressions that are hard to judge, may be subject to penalties such as removal or strikes if management, considering all circumstances, determines them to be inappropriate (sensitive), for example because they are persistently repeated, the surrounding context shows malicious intent, or other participants find them uncomfortable.

## Spoiler tags

Spoiler tags are not a means of permitting prohibited R-18, NSFW, grotesque, or other sensitive content. Prohibited content may not be posted whether or not it is hidden by a spoiler tag.
